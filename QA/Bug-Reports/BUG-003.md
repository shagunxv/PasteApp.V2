# BUG-003: Invalid Email Format Accepted During Registration

- **Module:** Authentication – Registration
- **Endpoint:** POST /api/register
- **Severity:** High
- **Priority:** High
- **Test Input:** `invalid-email`
- **Expected Result:** 400 Bad Request with an invalid email validation message.
- **Actual Result:** 201 Created. Account was successfully created.
- **Status:** Open

**Impact:** Users can register with incorrectly formatted email addresses, potentially causing problems with email communication and account management.
